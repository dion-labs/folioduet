Good fits

Documents worth spending time with

FolioDuet is designed for material you want to hear without giving up the structure and context of a page.

  • Long reports and research papers
  • Text-based books and manuscripts
  • Course readings with a selectable text layer
  • Drafts you want to review by ear and eye

Known limits

Clear boundaries, before you import

  • Image-only scans need OCR elsewhere first; FolioDuet requires a usable PDF text layer.
  • Extraction quality depends on the order and quality of the PDF’s embedded text.
  • Neural speech needs a network connection and availability from the configured provider.
  • Voice choice and native fallback behavior vary by browser and provider.

Listening, not file export: FolioDuet streams narration while you read. It does not currently export an MP3 or M4B file.

Frequently asked questions

PDF to audiobook questions

Can I turn a PDF into an audiobook for free?

You can try FolioDuet as a guest and import a text-based PDF without paying or creating an account. Sponsored Fish Audio availability may change. You can configure a supported provider or use a system voice when available; this is not a promise of permanently unlimited neural narration.

Can I download an MP3?

No. Playback currently streams inside the reader; FolioDuet does not export MP3 or M4B files.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

It works with PDFs that contain a usable text layer. OCR for image-only scans is not currently promised.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the sample or a local import. Signing in adds processed-text, library, preferences, and reading-progress sync.

Is my PDF uploaded?

The original PDF stays on the import device. Extracted text may sync when you sign in, and text selected for neural narration is processed by your configured speech provider.

Can I continue on another device?

Yes, after signing in, processed text and your reading position can sync. The original PDF binary is not copied to the other device.
